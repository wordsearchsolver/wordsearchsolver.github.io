Pro Advice

Tips for Solving Crosswords

Improve your crossword skills and get the most out of our solver.

1

Start with the Easiest Clues

Fill in the clues you're confident about first. These letters give you helpful constraints for the harder clues and reduce the number of possibilities.

2

Use the Pattern, Not Just the Clue

The clue tells you the meaning, but the pattern tells you the letters. Combine both. Enter every known letter into the solver to narrow down candidates dramatically.

3

Check the Word Length

Always verify the word length in the crossword grid. Use the length filter in the solver to avoid wasting time on words that don't fit.

4

Try Multiple Patterns

If one pattern gives too many results, add more known letters. If it gives none, remove a letter or two — you may have a wrong letter filled in.

5

Watch for Common Crossword Words

Crosswords use many abbreviations and common small words: ERA, ERE, ADO, ORE, ANTE, ETUI, OLEO, etc. Keep a mental list and try them when stuck.

6

Use the Solver as a Learning Tool

Don't just copy answers — study the results. Seeing how a word fits the pattern teaches you the crossword constructor's style and helps you solve future puzzles faster.

Background

What Is a Crossword Puzzle?

A crossword puzzle is a word game where you fill in a grid of squares with letters to form words, guided by clues. The words intersect with each other, so solving one clue gives you letters for crossing clues.

Crosswords were invented in the early 20th century and have become one of the most popular word puzzles worldwide. They come in many styles, from straightforward "quick" crosswords to the complex, cryptic crosswords popular in the UK.

Solving crosswords is a great way to build vocabulary, improve spelling, and keep your brain active. They're used in classrooms, therapy sessions, and as daily entertainment in newspapers around the world.

Our Crossword Puzzle Solver helps you when you're stuck. Enter the letters you know, use question marks for unknowns, and get a list of possible answers from our word database. It's like having a crossword dictionary in your pocket.
