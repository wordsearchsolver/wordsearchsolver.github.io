Tips for Better Results

Get the most out of the crossword solver and sharpen your own solving skills at the same time.

1

Count Your Squares First

Before typing a pattern, count the exact number of squares in the grid entry — the pattern length must match the answer length exactly.

2

Fill In Every Known Letter

Enter every letter you've already solved from crossing answers. The more letters you provide, the fewer — and more accurate — the matches.

3

Use "Must Contain" for Loose Clues

If a clue hints at a specific letter without confirming its position, add it under "Must Contain" instead of guessing at a blank.

4

Too Many Results? Narrow the Pattern

A pattern with many blanks can return hundreds of matches. Solve one or two crossing answers first to shrink the list.

5

Click to Copy Instantly

Click any word chip in the results to copy just that word — handy when you've spotted the right answer in a long list.

6

Manual Tip: Solve Crossings First

Fill in the answers you're most confident about first — their crossing letters often make neighboring clues far easier to crack.

7

Manual Tip: Watch for Plurals & Tenses

Clue tense and plurality usually match the answer — a clue ending in "-ed" often needs a past-tense word to fit the grid.

8

Load Sample to Learn the Format

Not sure how to format a pattern? Click "Load Sample" to see a working example, then swap in your own letters and blanks.

Background

What Is a Crossword Puzzle?

A crossword is a word puzzle made up of a grid of black and white squares. Clues are numbered and correspond to the "Across" and "Down" entries, and each answer intersects with others sharing common letters.

Solving a crossword clue usually means combining two things: the clue's wordplay or definition, and the letter pattern created by any answers you've already filled in around it.

Crosswords have been published since 1913 and remain one of the most popular word games in newspapers, magazines, and dedicated puzzle apps worldwide.

Our Crossword Solver handles the second half of that equation — given a pattern of known letters and blanks, it instantly returns every dictionary word that fits, so you can confirm your answer or get unstuck.
