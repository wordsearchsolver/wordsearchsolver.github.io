Hitting your own body ends the run. In Deadly mode, hitting a wall ends it too. In Wrap mode you reappear on the opposite side.

🎓 Snake Strategy Tips

  • Stay away from the edges early. Playing in the middle gives you room to turn, while walls remove half your options.
  • Plan two moves ahead. Before each turn, check where your tail will be, not just where it is now.
  • Build a loop pattern. When the snake gets long, sweep the board in rows so you never trap yourself in a pocket.
  • Don’t chase the apple head-on. A longer route that keeps your space open is safer than a straight line into a corner.
  • Try Wrap mode. Removing the walls makes the game more forgiving and is great for practising long-snake routes.

The Story Behind Snake

Snake is one of the best-known video game concepts. Versions of the idea date back to arcade games of the 1970s, and it became a household name when a version was included on Nokia mobile phones in the late 1990s. The appeal is simple: the better you do, the harder the game becomes, because your own body turns into the obstacle.

This version keeps the rules faithful. You control the direction of the snake and nothing else, so every run is a test of planning and reflexes. A 20 by 20 grid gives enough room for long runs while keeping each decision quick.

The game runs entirely in your browser with no download. If a network blocks some websites, whether this page opens depends on that network’s own rules.
