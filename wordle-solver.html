🎓 Wordle Strategy Tips

  • Start with common letters. Openers rich in vowels and frequent consonants, such as ones containing R, S, T, L, N and E, tend to reveal more on the first turn.
  • Use your second guess to explore. Choose a word with five new letters, even if it can’t be the answer, when your first guess found little.
  • Beware of word families. If you have _IGHT or _OUND, many words fit. Spend a guess on a word that tests several starting letters at once.
  • Remember repeated letters. A gray tile for a letter you have already placed can mean the word contains that letter only once.
  • Play the game, then check. Try thinking first and use the helper only when you are stuck for a better experience.

How a Wordle Solver Works

Wordle gives you coloured feedback on each guess: green for the right letter in the right place, yellow for a letter in the word but in a different spot, and gray for a letter that is absent, or that appears fewer times than you guessed it. A solver reverses that process. For every word in its dictionary, it asks: if this were the answer, would my guesses have produced exactly the colours I saw? Words that would not are discarded.

What remains is the list of possible answers. To suggest a next guess, the solver counts how often each letter shows up, overall and in each position, across the remaining words, then favours words that use those common letters and avoid wasting a slot on a repeat. It is a simple, transparent heuristic rather than a guaranteed optimal strategy, but it narrows the field quickly.
